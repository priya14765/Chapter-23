Chapter-23 · The Archival Keepsake 💌✨
A tactile digital heirloom celebrating twenty-three years of shared life, laughter, and devotion. 🌿💍🕊️

The Story 📖🕯️
True companionship isn't measured simply in dates on a calendar, but in the texture of ordinary days: shared evening cups of chai ☕, long rides down coastal roads 🚲🌊, quiet temple visits at the break of dawn 🛕🌸, and the unshakeable foundation built for a family. 🏡❤️

Chapter-23 was crafted to be far warmer and more soulful than a standard photo gallery. Designed as a living digital time capsule, it blends old-world nostalgia—crimson wax seals, aged parchment textures, polaroid frames, and vintage postage stamps—with seamless modern interactions. 📜✨ Every single memory is tucked safely away, waiting to be gently unlocked, one chapter at a time. 🗝️

What Awaits Inside 🎁✨
The Wax Seal Ritual 🕯️🔥

The journey doesn't begin with an ordinary click; it begins with an intention. Guests break a heavy wax seal on an embossed vintage envelope with a patient long-press, scattering golden sparks and unveiling 23 years of love. 🌟✉️

Opening the Keepsake 🚀📂
No build steps, external servers, or heavy frameworks required—pure web craftsmanship! 💻✨

Place your media assets in their respective folders:

📁 Images/Moments/ — Named milestone portraits (Anniversary.jpg, Beach.jpg, Temple.jpg, etc.)

📁 Images/ — Numbered family candids (1.jpg to 25.jpg)

🎵 Root folder — Audio1.mp3

Open index.html in any desktop or mobile browser. 🌐📱

Press and hold the wax seal on the envelope to begin the journey! 💌🕊️
